01

UC IRVINE · DEEP LEARNING · HARDWARE ANALYSIS

Deep Learning Accelerator Co-Optimization

Goal
Explore hardware and dataflow optimization for VGG16 and UNet under resource constraints, using MAESTRO to evaluate design choices.
My role
I was primarily responsible for optimizing the VGG16 accelerator configuration. I also contributed to UNet optimization by analyzing and refining dataflow mappings and adjusting processing element (PE) counts and network-on-chip (NoC) bandwidth.
Outcome
The VGG16 configuration met the following targets in MAESTRO-based evaluation.
VGG16 latency≤ 200 ms
VGG16 energy≤ 240 mJ

Results from MAESTRO-based evaluation, rather than measurements from a fabricated chip.

Approach & trade-offs

I explored spatial and temporal dataflow mappings and hardware parameters to balance resource use, latency, bandwidth, and energy consumption. The work involved comparing candidate configurations and refining them against the project constraints.

MAESTRO · VGG16 · UNet · Dataflow mapping

02

UALBANY · EMBEDDED SYSTEMS · TEAM CAPSTONE

Wireless LED Configuration Prototype

Goal
Explore wireless configuration of LED lighting, with settings stored while powered off and applied after power was restored.
My role
I built the initial prototype and adapted existing example code, combining LED control and NFC functionality on Arduino. I compiled component and cost information and recorded a demonstration video. I also explored integrating the NFC component into a PCB design and participated in hardware soldering.
Outcome
A prototype that could store settings while powered off and apply them after power restoration, documented with an LED mode changing demonstration.
